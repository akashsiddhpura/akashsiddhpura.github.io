# Overview

The Contact section is the final step in the visitor journey.

After learning about Aakash's experience, engineering philosophy, projects, and achievements, visitors should feel confident reaching out.

The objective is not simply to provide contact information.

The objective is to create a meaningful opportunity for collaboration.

This section should feel welcoming, professional, and human.

---

# Objective

Answer one question:

"Why should I contact Aakash today?"

The section should remove hesitation and encourage visitors to start a conversation.

Whether it's a startup founder, recruiter, engineering manager, or freelance client, everyone should feel that reaching out is worthwhile.

---

# Visitor Emotion

Visitors should feel:

• Confidence

• Excitement

• Trust

• Comfort

Visitors should think:

"This is exactly the engineer I want to work with."

# Design Notes

Use a two-column layout.

Left

Conversation

Story

Availability

Right

Premium Contact Form

Use glass cards with subtle gradients.

Avoid generic contact layouts.

The section should feel warm and approachable while maintaining a premium aesthetic.

---

# Animation Notes

Section fades into view.

Form fields animate with subtle stagger.

Cards gently elevate on hover.

Submit button provides satisfying feedback.

Success state should animate smoothly.

---

# Accessibility

All form fields require labels.

Support keyboard navigation.

Clear validation messages.

Proper focus indicators.

High color contrast.

ARIA-compliant form controls.
