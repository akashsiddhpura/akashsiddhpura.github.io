# Overview

The footer is the final touchpoint of the portfolio.

Rather than acting as a simple copyright section, it should reinforce Aakash's personal brand, encourage meaningful connections, and provide quick access to important resources.

The footer should feel elegant, minimal, and intentional.

Visitors should leave the website with a lasting impression of professionalism and craftsmanship.

---

# Objective

Answer one final question:

"What's the next step?"

Whether visitors want to connect, revisit projects, download the resume, or explore GitHub, the footer should make every important destination easily accessible.

---

# Visitor Emotion

Visitors should feel:

• Appreciation

• Trust

• Professionalism

• Completion

Visitors should think:

"I'll definitely remember this portfolio."

# Design Notes

The footer should feel like a natural continuation of the portfolio.

Use generous spacing.

Avoid large blocks of text.

Navigation should remain clean.

Keep visual hierarchy simple.

---

# Animation Notes

Footer fades into view.

Social icons gently scale on hover.

Buttons have subtle lift.

Background glow moves slowly.

No distracting animations.

---

# Accessibility

All navigation links should be keyboard accessible.

Icons require descriptive labels.

Maintain WCAG AA contrast.

Respect reduced motion preferences.

---
