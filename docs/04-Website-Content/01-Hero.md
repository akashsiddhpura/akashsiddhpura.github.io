# Overview

The Hero section is the most important part of the portfolio.

Visitors should understand who Aakash is, what he builds, and why they should continue exploring within the first five seconds.

The Hero should create an emotional response before communicating technical details.

It should immediately feel premium, modern, and professionally crafted.

---

# Hero Objective

The Hero exists to answer four questions immediately.

Who is Aakash?

↓

What does he do?

↓

Why should I trust him?

↓

Why should I continue scrolling?

---

# First Impression Goals

Within 3 seconds

The visitor should think:

"This feels premium."

---

Within 5 seconds

The visitor should think:

"This engineer builds serious products."

---

Within 10 seconds

The visitor should think:

"I want to see his work."

---

# Emotional Goals

Visitors should feel:

- Confidence
- Curiosity
- Professionalism
- Trust
- Inspiration

The Hero should never feel:

- Loud
- Generic
- Template based
- Flashy
- Sales driven

# Headline

The headline should be short, memorable, and timeless.

Recommended Headline

Building software that scales, performs, and feels effortless to use.

Alternative Headlines

Engineering mobile products people love to use.

Transforming ambitious ideas into production-ready software.

Crafting digital experiences that users love and businesses trust.

Building products where architecture meets experience.

---

# Supporting Copy

The supporting paragraph should communicate product thinking rather than listing technologies.

Recommended Copy

Software Engineer specializing in mobile product engineering, scalable architecture, and exceptional user experiences.

I partner with startups and product teams to transform ambitious ideas into reliable, production-ready applications.

# Background

The Hero background should be subtle.

Include:

- Soft gradient mesh
- Ambient glow
- Noise texture
- Floating blurred circles
- Minimal particle movement

Avoid distracting visuals.

# Content Rules

The Hero should avoid:

Large paragraphs

Technology lists

Buzzwords

Progress bars

Typing animations

Excessive movement

---

# Hero Copy Principles

Write like an experienced engineer.

Avoid:

Passionate

Hardworking

Dedicated

Self-motivated

Team player

Instead show credibility through:

Projects

Architecture

Achievements

Business impact

---

# Accessibility

Buttons must be keyboard accessible.

Typography must maintain contrast.

Animations must support reduced motion.

All content should remain readable on mobile.

---

# Success Criteria

The Hero is successful if visitors immediately understand:

Who Aakash is.

What he builds.

Why his work is valuable.

Why they should continue scrolling.

---

# Final Principle

The Hero should never try to impress with effects.

It should earn trust through clarity, confidence, craftsmanship, and thoughtful presentation.

The best compliment after viewing the Hero should be:

"This is exactly the kind of engineer I'd trust to build our product."
