# Overview

The Trust Indicators section appears immediately after the Hero section.

Its purpose is to reinforce credibility before visitors read about experience or projects.

Rather than asking visitors to trust claims, this section demonstrates credibility using measurable achievements and concise highlights.

The section should be highly visual, easy to scan, and completed within a few seconds.

---

# Objective

The objective of this section is to answer one question:

"Why should I trust this engineer?"

Within five seconds, visitors should understand that Aakash has real production experience, has worked on products at scale, and focuses on engineering quality.

---

# Visitor Emotion

Visitors should feel:

- Confidence
- Curiosity
- Trust

Visitors should think:

"This engineer has built real products."

Instead of listing technologies, highlight outcomes.

Examples

---

# Content Rules

Keep text short.

Maximum two lines per card.

Use numbers wherever possible.

Avoid long explanations.

Avoid technical jargon.

Avoid technology logos.

Focus on outcomes.

---

# Success Criteria

The section is successful if visitors immediately understand:

- Aakash has real production experience.
- His work has reached real users.
- He focuses on quality, performance, and scalable engineering.
- The portfolio is backed by measurable impact rather than generic claims.

---

# Final Principle

Trust should be earned through evidence.

Every statistic and highlight should represent real work and meaningful engineering outcomes.

The objective is not to impress with numbers.

The objective is to demonstrate credibility through measurable experience.
