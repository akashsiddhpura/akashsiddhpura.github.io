# Overview

This document defines the brand identity, positioning, personality, and communication style for Aakash Siddhpura's personal portfolio website.

The portfolio is not intended to function as an online resume. Instead, it should communicate engineering excellence, product thinking, and craftsmanship through thoughtful storytelling, premium design, and interactive experiences.

Every design decision, animation, section, and piece of content should align with the brand defined in this document.

---

# Mission

Create a portfolio that demonstrates the ability to build world-class digital products while showcasing technical expertise, design appreciation, and product ownership.

The portfolio should leave visitors with the impression that Aakash is capable of leading product development from idea to production.

---

# Vision

Become recognized as an engineer who combines software engineering, product thinking, and user experience to build meaningful digital products.

The website should feel like a premium software product rather than a traditional developer portfolio.

---
