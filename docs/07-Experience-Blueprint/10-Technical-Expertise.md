# Overview

The Technical Expertise section showcases the engineering capabilities that support the products I build.

Rather than presenting a collection of technologies, this section explains the areas in which I have developed deep practical expertise through real-world product development.

Every skill should represent production experience—not simply familiarity.

---

# Purpose

This section answers:

- What technologies do I work with?
- Where is my strongest expertise?
- What engineering problems can I solve?
- What value do I bring to a product team?

---

# Business Goal

Help technical recruiters and engineering managers quickly evaluate my technical strengths.

Demonstrate depth instead of breadth.

Position me as an engineer capable of owning complete product development.

---

# Visitor Emotion

Visitors should think:

✓ Strong technical foundation.

✓ Modern engineering practices.

✓ Production experience.

✓ Can own complex mobile products.

Never:

✗ Technology collector.

✗ Buzzword-heavy.

✗ Generic skills section.
