> Version: 1.0
> Owner: Aakash Siddhpura
> Project: Portfolio v2
> Last Updated: July 2026

---

# Overview

The Contact section is the final and most important conversion point of the portfolio.

By the time visitors reach this section, they already understand who I am, how I think, and the products I have built.

The goal is no longer to prove my abilities.

The goal is to make starting a conversation feel easy, welcoming, and professional.

The experience should feel like the beginning of a collaboration rather than the end of a portfolio.

---

# Purpose

This section answers:

- How can I contact Aakash?
- Is he available?
- What kind of work is he interested in?
- What's the best way to reach him?
