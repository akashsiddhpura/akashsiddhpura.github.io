# Trust Indicators Experience Blueprint

> Version: 1.0
> Owner: Aakash Siddhpura
> Project: Portfolio v2
> Last Updated: July 2026

---

# Overview

Trust Indicators are the bridge between the Hero and the About section.

Visitors have already learned who I am.

Now they need evidence that my experience is real.

This section should communicate credibility through concise, meaningful proof points rather than exaggerated claims.

The design should feel subtle and confident.

---

# Purpose

The section exists to answer:

"Why should I trust this engineer?"

Rather than listing technologies or achievements, this section should communicate experience through carefully selected metrics and highlights.

---

# Business Goal

Increase visitor confidence before they continue into the portfolio.

Encourage visitors to explore projects and experience.

Reduce uncertainty.

Strengthen credibility.

---

# Visitor Emotion

Visitors should feel:

✓ Confident

✓ Curious

✓ Reassured

✓ Interested

Never:

✗ Overwhelmed

✗ Distracted

✗ Sold to

---

# Reading Time

5–10 seconds

The section should be understandable at a glance.

  [ Experience ]

  [ Industries ]

  [ Architecture ]

Four equally sized cards.

# Mobile Layout

Two columns.

No horizontal scrolling.

# Card Design

Background

Subtle glass surface or elevated card.

Border

Very light.

Radius

20px

Padding

28–32px

# Typography

Metric

36–42px

Bold

Title

16px

SemiBold

Description

15px

Regular

# Icons

Use simple outline icons from Lucide.

Examples:

- Briefcase
- Layers
- Smartphone
- Rocket
- Shield
- Zap

Icons should support content, not dominate it.

---

# Visual Hierarchy

Each card follows:

Icon

↓

Primary Metric

↓

Title

↓

Description

The metric should attract attention first.

---

# Hover Interaction

Desktop only.

Card lifts slightly.

Border brightens.

Background becomes subtly lighter.

Duration

180–220ms

No dramatic scaling.

---

# Animation

Cards appear after the Hero.

Sequence:

Fade

↓

Translate Up

↓

Stagger

Delay

80ms between cards.

Cards animate only once.

---

# Responsive Behaviour

Desktop

4 Columns

Laptop

4 Columns

Tablet

2 × 2 Grid

Mobile

2 Columns

Maintain equal spacing.

---

# Accessibility

Cards are informational.

No interaction required.

Maintain sufficient color contrast.

Readable typography.

---

# Performance

Animations should use:

Transform

Opacity

Avoid layout recalculation.

Target 60 FPS.

- Certifications
- Client Testimonials
- Community Contributions

---

# Success Criteria

Visitors should immediately understand:

✓ Experienced engineer

✓ Production experience

✓ Multiple industries

✓ Strong engineering mindset

Without reading long paragraphs.

# Final Principle

Trust should be demonstrated through meaningful evidence rather than exaggerated claims.

Every number, statement, and highlight should be truthful, relevant, and easy to understand.

This section should quietly reinforce confidence before visitors continue exploring the portfolio.
