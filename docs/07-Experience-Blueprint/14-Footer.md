# Footer Experience Blueprint

> Version: 1.0
> Owner: Aakash Siddhpura
> Project: Portfolio v2
> Last Updated: July 2026

---

# Overview

The Footer is the final chapter of the portfolio experience.

Rather than acting as a legal or navigation section, the footer should provide a calm ending that reinforces the portfolio's message and encourages visitors to stay connected.

It should feel intentional, minimal, and memorable.

Visitors should leave with the same impression they had in the Hero:

This engineer cares about quality.

---

# Purpose

The Footer exists to:

- End the experience gracefully.
- Reinforce personal branding.
- Provide quick navigation.
- Offer important links.
- Leave visitors with a memorable final message.

---

# Business Goal

Increase:

- Resume downloads
- GitHub visits
- LinkedIn connections
- Return visitors

Leave a lasting professional impression.

# Final Principle

The Footer should never feel like the end of a webpage.

It should feel like the final sentence of a great conversation.
