# Overview

The Currently Building section provides a snapshot of what I'm actively exploring, learning, and building beyond my day-to-day work.

Rather than presenting completed achievements, this section highlights my curiosity, long-term vision, and commitment to continuous improvement.

Visitors should leave with the impression that I am constantly evolving as an engineer.

---

# Section Purpose

This section answers:

- What am I currently learning?
- Where am I investing my time?
- What excites me outside daily work?
- What direction is my career heading?

---

# Business Goal

Show continuous growth.

Demonstrate curiosity.

Position me as someone who learns proactively.

Create opportunities for conversations during interviews.

---

# Visitor Emotion

Visitors should feel:

✓ Curious

✓ Inspired

✓ Forward-thinking

✓ Motivated

Never:

✗ Incomplete

✗ Random

✗ Like a wishlist
