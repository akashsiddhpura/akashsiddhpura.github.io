# Overview

The Experience section demonstrates professional growth through meaningful responsibilities, product ownership, and engineering impact.

Rather than acting as a chronological resume, this section should communicate progression—from building features to designing architecture, mentoring developers, and leading technical decisions.

Visitors should understand how my responsibilities have evolved throughout my career.

---

# Section Purpose

This section answers:

- Where have I worked?
- What products have I built?
- How have my responsibilities evolved?
- What level of ownership do I have?
- What business impact have I delivered?

---

# Business Goal

Position me as a Senior Software Engineer capable of:

- Owning product development
- Leading technical initiatives
- Designing scalable architectures
- Collaborating across teams
- Delivering production-ready software

---

# Visitor Emotion

Visitors should feel:

✓ Experienced

✓ Reliable

✓ Product-focused

✓ Trusted with responsibility

Not:

✗ "He changed jobs."

Instead:

✓ "His responsibilities kept growing."
