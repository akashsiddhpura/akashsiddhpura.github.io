---

# Overview

Mobile is not a secondary experience.

More than half of visitors will likely view the portfolio on a mobile device. The mobile experience should be intentionally designed rather than simply adapted from desktop.

Every interaction should feel native, fast, and effortless.

---

# Experience Goals

The mobile experience should:

- Feel like a premium mobile application.
- Prioritize readability.
- Maintain the same emotional journey as desktop.
- Keep interactions thumb-friendly.
- Reduce unnecessary scrolling.
- Preserve visual hierarchy.

---

# Design Philosophy

Mobile is not desktop scaled down.

Every section should be redesigned where necessary.

Layouts should prioritize:

- Simplicity
- Readability
- Performance
- Touch interaction

# Design Decisions

## Decision 01

Design for mobile first, not mobile later.

## Decision 02

Touch interaction always takes priority over visual effects.

## Decision 03

If an animation affects usability on mobile, remove it.

---

# Things to Avoid

- Tiny buttons
- Small typography
- Horizontal scrolling
- Heavy blur effects
- Complex cursor effects
- Desktop layouts forced onto mobile

---
