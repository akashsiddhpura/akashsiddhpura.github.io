# Featured Projects Experience Blueprint

> Version: 1.0
> Owner: Aakash Siddhpura
> Project: Portfolio v2
> Last Updated: July 2026

---

# Overview

The Featured Projects section is the centerpiece of the portfolio.

This is where visitors move from believing my claims to seeing proof of my engineering ability.

Projects should not be presented as a gallery.

Each project should feel like a premium product with its own story, engineering challenges, business value, and technical decisions.

The goal is to encourage visitors to explore deeper rather than simply skim through screenshots.

---

# Section Purpose

This section exists to answer:

- What products has Aakash built?
- How complex are those products?
- What engineering problems has he solved?
- What level of ownership did he have?
- Would I trust him to build my product?

---

# Business Goal

Position me as an engineer who:

- Builds complete products
- Solves real business problems
- Thinks beyond implementation
- Understands architecture
- Delivers polished user experiences

The projects should become the strongest reason to contact me.

---

# Visitor Emotion

Visitors should feel:

✓ These are real production products.

✓ He solves difficult engineering problems.

✓ He builds polished software.

✓ I'd like to know more.

Never:

✗ Another portfolio gallery

✗ Generic app screenshots

✗ Technology showcase

---

# Visual Style

Each project should have:

Large spacing

Premium typography

High-quality screenshots

Consistent device mockups

Soft shadows

Subtle gradients

Projects should feel like product landing pages rather than portfolio cards.

---

# Accessibility

Images include descriptive alt text.

CTA buttons keyboard accessible.

Maintain strong contrast.

Readable typography.

---

# Performance

Lazy load screenshots.

Use Next.js Image.

Preload first project image.

Use responsive image sizes.

Avoid autoplay videos.

---

# Responsive Behaviour

Desktop

Alternating layout.

Tablet

Image above content.

Mobile

Single-column cards.

Maintain readability and generous spacing.

# Success Criteria

Visitors should leave this section thinking:

✓ He builds real products.

✓ He solves difficult problems.

# Final Principle

Projects should never feel like portfolio entries.

They should feel like products with stories.

Every project should communicate business value, engineering depth, and thoughtful execution before mentioning technologies.

The objective is not to impress visitors with features.

The objective is to build confidence through craftsmanship.
