> Version: 1.0
> Owner: Aakash Siddhpura

> Last Updated: July 2026

---

# Overview

The Achievements section celebrates meaningful milestones throughout my engineering journey.

Rather than focusing on awards or certifications, this section highlights the moments that demonstrate growth, responsibility, continuous learning, and engineering impact.

Every achievement should reinforce the trust built throughout the portfolio.

---

# Purpose

This section answers:

- What am I proud of?
- How have I grown as an engineer?
- What meaningful milestones have I achieved?
- Why should someone trust my experience?

---

# Business Goal

Strengthen credibility.

Reinforce technical maturity.

Show continuous progression.

Create confidence before visitors reach the contact section.

---

# Visitor Emotion

Visitors should feel:

✓ Consistent growth

✓ Strong ownership

✓ Continuous learning

✓ Product mindset

Not:

✗ Bragging

✗ Self-promotion

✗ Resume bullets

Visitors should leave this section believing:

✓ He takes ownership.

# Final Principle

Achievements are not measured by awards.

This section should celebrate growth through engineering rather than recognition through titles.
